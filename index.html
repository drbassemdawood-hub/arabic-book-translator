How it works

    
    
      
  • Costs come from your own Anthropic account. Before translating, the log shows an
        estimated cost and time for the whole book. Rough guide with default settings (Opus, two-pass review):
        a 300-page book ≈ $15–25; a 2000-page book ≈ $100–170. Turn off the review pass or choose Sonnet to cut this substantially.
    • 
      
  • Interruptions never lose paid work. Progress is saved in your browser after every passage.
        If you cancel, lose connection, or close the tab — press Start again with the same book and it resumes.
    • 
      
  • Scanned PDFs (no text layer) are detected automatically and read with Claude vision OCR;
        the estimated OCR cost is shown first, and results are cached per page.
    • 
      
  • Careful speed translates one passage at a time, feeding each passage the previous Arabic
        text — best consistency of names and style. Fast/Fastest run 3 or 6 passages in parallel
        (needs an API rate-limit tier that allows it).
    • 
      
  • Translated books are for your personal use — respect the copyright of the original work.
